Bioregion colours now come from @mapequation/c3 2 (OKLab-based): colours are more evenly distinct, the Greys scheme no longer gives every bioregion the same grey, and the colour settings swap the HSL saturation range for a Tone choice and an OKLCH lightness range. The default palette is now c3's Oklch hue circle with the strong tone, and a new Colors section in the sidebar holds the palette, tone and the other colour options, with a preview of the bioregion colours.
